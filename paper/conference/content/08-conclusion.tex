\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

A redaction tool given to an LLM agent is a channel as well as a filter. In the guardrail we studied, a reply documented as content-free returned person names through detector metadata. An allow-list and a planted-value property test closed that channel. A regex-gated neural tier raised person-name recall on real user-generated text from nothing to about half, but the gate that keeps it cheap leaves a quarter of the achievable recall on the table. Batched inference re-opened the chunking leak that the streaming engine exists to close. And a transport detail, Nagle's algorithm, cost more per REST call than detection did for small inputs. Each finding came from measuring the tool from the agent's side of the boundary, and we recommend that every agent-facing redaction tool be held to such a test.
