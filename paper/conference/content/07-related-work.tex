\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{De-identification and named-entity recognition} Rule-based and neural de-identification of clinical text \cite{norgeot2020philter,dernoncourt2017deid}, neural entity recognition in security text \cite{simran2020cybersecner}, and toolkits such as Presidio \cite{presidio,presidioresearch} combine patterns with statistical or transformer models \cite{devlin2019bert,sanh2019distilbert,zaratiana2024gliner}. Lison et al.\ argue that detection alone is not anonymisation \cite{lison2021anonymisation}, and the Text Anonymization Benchmark measures disclosure risk on court judgments \cite{pilan2022tab}. WNUT-17 targets the novel, rare and lower-case entities of user-generated text \cite{derczynski2017wnut}, which is why we use it as the real corpus here.

\paragraph{Secrets} Credential leakage in public repositories is widespread \cite{meli2019git}. Scanners such as gitleaks and TruffleHog \cite{gitleaks,trufflehog} differ mainly in their rules and filters \cite{basak2023comparative}. They report findings to people, not to a model.

\paragraph{LLM agents and privacy} Prompt injection lets content read by an agent steer its tool calls \cite{greshake2023injection}, and AgentDojo evaluates such attacks and defences in tool-using agents \cite{debenedetti2024agentdojo}. Language models can memorise and emit training data, including personal data \cite{carlini2021extracting,lukas2023pii}. Those works study the model. We study a tool on the boundary: what its replies disclose, independent of the model. MCP \cite{mcpspec} standardises how agents call such tools, but does not define what a tool may disclose.

\paragraph{Streaming redaction} Splitting input into pieces is a known way to evade detection \cite{ptacek1998insertion}, and streaming regular-expression engines carry automaton state across blocks to avoid it \cite{wang2019hyperscan}. SafeStream's engine instead keeps an overlap whose length each tier declares; we rely on it and test whether a neural tier respects the bound it declares.
