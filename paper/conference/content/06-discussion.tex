\section{Discussion and limitations}\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Contracts need tests that plant values} The leak was in a reply documented as content-free, and its existing test passed because the tiers it used held no input text in metadata. A deny-list makes every new detector a potential channel; an allow-list with a planted-value property test reverses the default.

\paragraph{A cheap gate is a recall decision} The gate makes the neural tier affordable on machine text (it reads about a tenth of a log) but caps recall at the share of names it can see, about 0.6 of names in real user-generated text. Where agents read chat logs, tickets or social-media exports, running the model on every line costs about three times as much and recovers about a quarter of person-name recall. That is a deployment choice the tool should state, not hide behind a default.

\paragraph{Batching and declared bounds} Batched INT8 inference is faster on long inputs, but because quantisation ranges depend on the rest of the batch, it breaks the property that makes streaming safe, and in our runs it left names in clear. A tier that declares its context must also be held to it. Here the pipeline's larger overlap hid an inaccurate declaration, which is luck, not design.

\paragraph{Restore is a capability, not a convenience} Reversible pseudonymisation is useful, but a restore call exposed to the agent makes the model the party that holds the key. Restoration belongs outside the agent's tool surface: in the client, after the model's reply and under the user's control.

\paragraph{Disclosure} We are informing the maintainers of T1 to T4 privately, with the reproducers, before naming them. The tools will be named in the final version of this paper, after their maintainers have had time to respond.

\paragraph{Limitations} The agent experiment used two open-weight models of 7 and 8 billion parameters, two injection wordings and 20 tickets per cell, and the second model did not engage with tool output at all; frontier models may resist these injections more, or call restore tools more readily. AgentTrace is synthetic, and its planted formats come from the rules' authors, so it supports no accuracy claim. WNUT-17 is small (129 documents) and its documents are groups of ten sentences, not natural documents. CoNLL-2003 is the model's training distribution. We evaluated one neural model, one quantisation scheme and one machine, and only four third-party tools: those that met our criteria on one day's search. Offsets, lengths and mask suffixes are disclosed by design; we described what they reveal but did not quantify re-identification from them. Egress was searched in five encodings, and an encoding outside that set would not be found.
