# Superseded: do not submit

This manuscript is an earlier version of the SafeStream-Redactor work and is
**superseded by `paper/ijis/`**, the version prepared for submission to the
*International Journal of Information Security* (Springer).

It reports the same system and overlapping results, and it does not contain the
later evaluations (TAB court judgments, Wikipedia biographies, CredData, the
held-out Linux logs and the free-text configuration). Submitting it to any venue
while `paper/ijis/` is under review, or after it is published without disclosing
the overlap, would be duplicate submission.

It is kept unchanged for the record only.
