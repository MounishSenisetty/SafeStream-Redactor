\item \textbf{Ethics approval and consent to participate.} Not applicable: no human participants were involved and the authors collected no data. presidio-research is synthetic, the secrets corpus consists of example strings from an open-source rule set together with generated negatives, RedactionBench is a public dataset released by its authors under CC BY 4.0, which they describe as seeded from real-world sources; the Loghub logs, the TAB court judgments and the Wikipedia biographies are public research datasets used under their authors' terms. CredData was built with its authors' script, which fetches public repositories and replaces every real credential with a random value of the same shape; the raw checkouts were deleted as soon as it had done so, and only obfuscated values were evaluated. All processing was local; no excerpt of any document and no credential value is published here, and no credential was sent to a live service (TruffleHog ran without verification).
\item \textbf{Consent for publication.} Not applicable.
\item \textbf{Data availability.} presidio-research, Nosey Parker and Loghub are public (Loghub is free for research use with citation of \cite{zhu2023loghub}); RedactionBench is available from its authors under CC BY 4.0; TAB \cite{pilan2022tab} is public under the MIT licence; the annotated Wikipedia biographies \cite{papadopoulou2022bootstrapping} are public from their authors; CredData \cite{creddata2021} is rebuilt from public repositories with its authors' script. The template-slot labels for the Loghub corpora are in the repository. Generated non-secrets are produced by the released code with a fixed seed. Split manifests, raw results and per-document predictions (character offsets only, no corpus text) are in the public repository under \texttt{research/results/}.
