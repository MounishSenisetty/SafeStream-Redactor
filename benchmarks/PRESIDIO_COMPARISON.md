# SafeStream-Redactor vs Microsoft Presidio

Generated by `benchmarks/presidio_compare.py` (Python 3.11, x86_64 Linux, 4 vCPU):
SafeStream-Redactor 0.4.0 from this branch, Presidio `presidio-analyzer` 2.2.364 with
its default `AnalyzerEngine()` (every recognizer, spaCy `en_core_web_lg`). Each tool
runs in its own process. Entity recall counts a gold span only when **every**
character of it is redacted; character precision/recall are over the union of gold
characters. "Tool RSS" is the process's peak RSS minus what it held after loading the
dataset (pyarrow included, for RedactionBench).

## Reading it

**Structured PII in noisy logs (`logs`).** On the adversarial log corpus (1 MB of
log/JSON/CSV/SQL lines dense with order numbers, timestamps, UUIDs, invalid SSNs and
version strings), SafeStream catches every gold email, phone, card, SSN and IPv4 with
0.999 character precision; Presidio (restricted to the same five entity types)
catches 86 % with 0.74 precision. **Caveat:** this corpus is generated by this repo
(`generate_hard_dataset.py`) in the formats SafeStream targets. It measures format
coverage and false-positive resistance, not real-world recall.

**Real documents (`redactionbench`).** On 200 RedactionBench documents (names,
addresses, credentials, IDs; each tool in its full default configuration, scored
type-agnostically) neither tool is close to complete:

- SafeStream (no NER) redacts **more precisely** (character precision 0.75 vs 0.40)
  and covers a **larger share of the sensitive characters** (0.41 vs 0.37), but
  fully catches fewer individual entities (0.44 vs 0.64). Most of its misses are
  names and free-text addresses, which a pattern engine does not see.
- With the spaCy NER tier (`en_core_web_sm`), SafeStream's entity recall rises to
  0.52 and character recall to 0.54, at 0.55 precision: NER redacts many capitalised
  words that are not personal data.
- Presidio's NER catches more names (entity recall 0.64), but also tags far more
  non-sensitive text (precision 0.40).

**Cost.** SafeStream's native core analyses text 250-450x faster than Presidio here
(5-9 MB/s vs 0.02 MB/s) in about 30 MB instead of about 840 MB. Adding the spaCy tier
makes SafeStream as slow and as large as Presidio: the model, not the engine, sets
the cost.

The pure-Python SafeStream rows equal the native ones exactly (same detections): the
backends differ only in speed.
